## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Specifically authored at the concept/hero level, generic-competent at the component level. The numbered-section register, spine timeline, ruled education list, and especially the hero's ledger-row canvas (real line numbers, cursor-tracked "active cell") are artifacts of someone who actually builds indices — not swappable to an unrelated product. Below the hero, `.card`/`.btn-primary`/pill-tag/project-grid vocabulary reverts to Linear/Stripe-issue components. Intentional trade-off per the CSS's own design contract, but it front-loads the "authored" feeling and lets it fade on scroll.

**Deterministic scan**: 3 findings, all warning-severity — overused font (Inter, a stated brief exception), em-dash overuse (15-16 raw count), marketing buzzword ("cutting-edge research," verbatim from the real resume). No new issues beyond prior runs.

**False positive identified**: the em-dash count is inflated by counting date-range dashes ("Aug 2024 — Present" ×8+) as prose padding. Only ~3 of 15-16 are genuine sentence-level em-dashes. The finding's severity is overstated by raw count alone.

## Overall Impression

The site earned real, specific character at the hero — genuinely uncommon for a CV site. What's left is making that character survive the scroll, and closing two real defects the assessments caught: the interactive hero effect can visually collide with the person's own name, and the one project that's supposed to read as the standout doesn't.

## What's Working

- **Hero canvas fidelity**: real line-number labels, opacity easing, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` bailout that fully disables the RAF loop — considered, not decorative.
- **Timeline spine**: a genuine non-card alternative to the generic "experience card grid," reinforcing subject matter through layout, not decoration.
- **Color system discipline**: two deliberately non-matched accents (olive/light, pink/dark), both independently verified AA-compliant (9.18:1 / 11.87:1 on their own backgrounds), still read as a coherent system.
- **Contrast, focus states, and responsive layout are clean across the board** — every text pairing passes WCAG AA, focus-visible rings cover all interactive elements, no horizontal overflow at 390px.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Hero interaction can occlude the name.** `#ledger-canvas` spans the full hero and its cursor-tracked "active cell" highlight draws directly over the H1 text when hovering near it (confirmed at cursor position over "Arroyave"). On a page whose first job is communicating a name, the signature interaction can visually fight the exact text it sits behind.
*Fix*: exclude the text column from the interactive highlight, or suppress/fade the cell fill when it overlaps the copy's bounding box.
*Suggested command*: `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] Featured project doesn't look featured.** DENi uses the same card treatment as the three secondary GitHub-link projects, differentiated only by a small badge pill and a plain secondary-styled button — no visual weight advantage matching its narrative importance as the flagship piece of evidence.
*Fix*: give it a distinct frame — larger treatment, a primary-styled CTA distinct from the secondary project links, or a preview element.
*Suggested command*: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Theme toggle has no announced state.** `aria-label="Toggle dark and light theme"` only, no `aria-pressed` bound to current theme — screen-reader users get an identical announcement regardless of state.
*Fix*: bind `aria-pressed="true|false"` in `theme.js`.
*Suggested command*: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Skills is the one section that breaks the page's own chunking discipline.** 4 tag-rows, 29 tags, fully expanded with no grouping beyond a label — every other section on the page chunks to ≤5 items; this one doesn't.
*Fix*: tighten grouping or introduce a consistent secondary structure (not a generic accordion — something in the ledger idiom).
*Suggested command*: `/impeccable layout`

**[P3] Primary nav omits 2 of 5 numbered sections.** Education and Skills are numbered like a table of contents but unreachable from the nav (only Experience/Projects/Contact are linked).
*Fix*: either link all five, or treat the numbering as pure decoration and stop implying it's a TOC.
*Suggested command*: `/impeccable clarify`

## Persona Red Flags

**Sam (accessibility-dependent)**: theme toggle's missing `aria-pressed` means state is invisible to screen readers; the site's signature hover interaction (the ledger active-cell) has zero non-visual equivalent — it's 100% invisible to Sam with no compensating content or alternative cue.

**Casey (distracted mobile user)**: at 390px, nav + theme toggle + Download CV render as a full-width wrapped button wall (~150px) above the hero photo before any headline text appears — Casey scrolls past a wall of chrome before reaching "who is this."

**Riley (deliberate stress tester)**: skills tag-wrap and long bullet lists held up fine under reflow/zoom testing; the one soft spot Riley would find immediately is hovering the hero canvas directly on the H1.

## Minor Observations

- Footer copyright year ("© 2026") is hardcoded, not computed — will go stale in Jan 2027.
- `og:image` is a portrait crop, fine for `summary` Twitter cards but cramped if a platform upgrades to `summary_large_image`.
- `.timeline-org` / `.project-card-link` rely on color+weight alone for link affordance with no hover-underline fallback.
- Light-mode `--text-tertiary` contrast (4.55:1) passes AA but sits only 0.05 above the 4.5:1 floor — worth a small safety margin.
- A full-page-screenshot "double sticky header" artifact was confirmed to be a screenshot-tool stitching quirk, not a real page defect.
